Description


A classic deviled eggs recipe. Full ingredients and step-by-step instructions


Ingridients


  • Eggs This recipe yields 12 deviled egg halves, so you’ll need six boiled eggs.
  • MayonnaiseMayonnaise makes the filling extra creamy
  • SugarA teaspoon of white sugar lends a hint of sweetness
  • VinegarA teaspoon of white vinegar, meanwhile, cuts the sweetness with acidity
  • MustardUse store-bought prepared mustard or homemade yellow mustard
  • VegetablesYou’ll need an onion and celery
  • SeasoningsThese deviled eggs are seasoned with salt and paprika

Steps


  1. Hard-boil the eggs. Boil 6 eggs using your preferred hard-boiled egg method. The recommended method takes about 50 minutes from beginning to end. Once done, cool and peel the eggs
  2. Halve the eggs. Slice each peeled egg in half lengthwise. This will give you 12 deviled egg halves
  3. Remove the yolks. Gently scoop or pop the yolks out of the egg white halves. Place the yolks in a mixing bowl and set the egg white halves aside on a serving platter
  4. Make the filling. Mash the yolks with a fork. Add mayonnaise for creaminess, 1 teaspoon white sugar, 1 teaspoon white vinegar, and prepared mustard. Mix until smooth and creamy
  5. Add vegetables and seasonings. Finely chop the onion and celery, then stir them into the yolk mixture. Season with salt and paprika to taste
  6. Adjust the flavor. Taste the filling and adjust as needed: add more mayonnaise for creaminess, vinegar for acidity, sugar for sweetness, or salt and paprika for seasoning
  7. Fill the egg whites. Spoon or pipe the yolk mixture into the egg white halves
  8. Garnish and serve. Sprinkle the filled eggs with a little paprika. Serve immediately, or refrigerate until ready to serve
